# Traits

Generated by tools/roster_export.gd from the trait scripts. Use these names and settings in the `traits` and `phases` columns.

## Enemy traits (enemies.csv)

### blink
Mid-dive it flickers, then teleports sideways. Counter: shoot it while it flickers, or aim where it lands (always `distance` to one side, toward the middle near the edges).

- `interval` = 1.4
- `telegraph` = 0.4
- `distance` = 120.0

### dash
Mid-dive it flashes, snaps sideways toward the player at high speed, then fires. Counter: the flash warns you, and it pauses after the dash.

- `interval` = 1.5
- `telegraph` = 0.3
- `dash_time` = 0.18
- `dash_speed` = 950.0

### mend
Every `interval` it repairs damaged enemies (and elites) within `radius`, with a visible beam. Counter: it is fragile, so kill it first.

- `radius` = 140.0
- `interval` = 2.0
- `amount` = 1
- `elite_amount` = 3

### mine
Lays up to `max_mines` slow drifting mines per dive. A mine costs a life on contact, but a shot sets it off and the blast wrecks nearby enemies. Counter: shoot mines next to enemy clusters.

- `interval` = 0.8
- `max_mines` = 3

### plate
Bolts an armor plate onto the enemy's front that soaks shots until it breaks. Counter: hit it from the side as it turns mid-dive, or break the plate first.


### rock_drop
Drops a rock once per dive, after it passes `drop_y`. The rock crushes any enemy it falls on and costs a life on contact. Counter: dodge it, and lure other enemies under it.

- `drop_y` = 300.0

### split
Bursts into `count` fast fragments when destroyed. Counter: piercing shots, or kill it high up so the fragments have farther to fly.

- `fragment` = fragment
- `count` = 2

## Elite and boss traits (elites.csv, bosses.csv)

### frost_shell
Wrapped in layers of ice that soak every hit. Heavy or piercing shots crack the ice faster, and the shell regrows a layer at a time when the player stops shooting it. While shelled it sheds rings of slow ice shards. Counter: keep the pressure on (or bring heavy/piercing guns), and weave through the rings.

- `layers` = 3
- `layer_hp` = 10
- `heavy_mult` = 3
- `regrow_delay` = 2.5
- `regrow_interval` = 1.2
- `ring_interval` = 4.5
- `ring_shards` = 12
- `ring_speed` = 110.0

### gravity
Its core drags the player sideways toward it and is shielded, except for `open_time` after each pulse (a ring of shots). Counter: fight the pull, and hit the core right after a pulse.

- `pull` = 95.0
- `closed_time` = 4.5
- `open_time` = 2.2
- `pulse_shots` = 12
- `pulse_speed` = 150.0

### hive_tow
Tows a hive pod that hatches a swarm of diving enemies every `spawn_interval`. Counter: destroy the pod first (it has its own hp); kill the carrier instead and the pod falls like a rock, crushing enemies below it.

- `pod_hp` = 30
- `swarm_enemy` = bee
- `swarm` = 3
- `spawn_interval` = 6.0
- `tether_length` = 120.0

### meteor_call
Every `interval` it marks `lanes` columns with warning stripes, then rains meteors down them. Meteors cost a life on contact and crush any enemy in the lane. Counter: stand outside the lanes, and lure enemies into them.

- `interval` = 7.0
- `warn_time` = 1.4
- `lanes` = 2
- `lane_width` = 70.0
- `meteors_per_lane` = 4
- `meteor_speed` = 460.0

### mirror
Raises a mirror that bounces the player's shots back at them. It glows before it rises and drops after `up_time`. Counter: hold fire while the mirror is up; hit it while it's down.

- `down_time` = 2.4
- `warn_time` = 0.6
- `up_time` = 2.6
- `reflect_speed` = 340.0
- `reflect_gap` = 0.12

### phase
Phases out (see-through, shots pass through), slides over the player, then snaps back solid and fires at once. Counter: it can only be hit while solid; the shimmer warns it is about to return.

- `solid_time` = 1.8
- `phase_time` = 2.0
- `chase_speed` = 240.0
- `phased_alpha` = 0.15

### puppeteer
Every `interval` it hooks strings onto up to `puppets` formation enemies, then hurls them at the player in a fast staggered volley. Counter: kill it and every puppet still diving breaks off; the strings show which enemies are coming; Cryo Pulse holds the volley.

- `puppets` = 6
- `interval` = 6.0
- `mark_time` = 1.0
- `stagger` = 0.15
- `puppet_brain` = puppet

### rock_tow
Tows a giant space rock on a tether below it. The rock blocks shots from below and takes a huge number of hits, breaking off falling chunks (each a hazard worth scrap) as it wears down. Counter: shoot the thin tether from an angle to drop the rock (the elite then fires faster), or grind the rock down and dodge the chunks.

- `rock_hp` = 240
- `chunks` = 6
- `tether_length` = 150.0
- `enraged_fire` = 0.55

### shield_link
Projects shield beams onto the nearest formation enemies, making them immune to shots. The elite itself is not shielded. Counter: kill the elite first; enemies that dive out of range lose their shield.

- `links` = 5
- `radius` = 300.0
- `refresh` = 0.4

### sweep
Every `interval` it stops, shows a thin aiming line, then sweeps a long laser across the screen below it. The beam costs a life on contact. Counter: watch the aiming line and get behind the sweep (it only goes one way), or freeze it.

- `interval` = 6.5
- `aim_time` = 1.1
- `sweep_time` = 1.6
- `arc` = 1.0

### twin
Comes as a linked pair. When one dies, the other rebuilds it after `revive_time` (up to `revives` times). Counter: kill both within the revive window; split your damage.

- `revive_time` = 3.0
- `revives` = 2
