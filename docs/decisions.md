# Decision log

Newest first. One line per decision with the reason.

- 2026-10-04: Roster expansion. Eric tuned the roster in an artifact first, then the saved sheet became the CSVs. Elite levels (elite_levels.csv): the StageRunner rolls a level per elite from the levels open on the stage; `Elite` runs per-level scaled copies of its traits (`EliteTrait.scaled`: counts and speeds x perk, waits / perk, hp settings x hp) so traits stay plain exported numbers and shared resources are never mutated. Hooks for new traits: `Bullet.weapon_id` (weakness, copy weapon), `Player.freeze_for` and `jam_random_slot` (time stop, jammer), `EnemyTrait.killed` (flagship, scrap thief), `Shield` loses `hitbox.damage` layers. Stage 3 holds the new enemies and elites so stages 1 and 2 keep their tuned pacing; the new bosses (Scatter Titan, Iron Fortress) are reachable with `?elite=` until a boss stage exists. Weakness uses weapon-id substrings (flak, rail, lance, missile, needle, twin, laser).
- 2026-10-03: Roster sheet (Eric picked the tuning sheet; must cover elite traits, new types and bosses). data/roster/enemies.csv, elites.csv and bosses.csv are the source of truth for numbers, looks and traits; `Roster` (autoload) applies them at startup over the cached .tres the stages use, and creates sheet-only types (stages reach them by `WaveDef.enemy_id`, `StageDef.elite_ids`/`elite_pool_ids`). Runtime instead of generating .tres, so an edit needs no tool run. Enemies and elites can carry several traits (per-trait state via `state()`); bosses are elites with `phases` (BossPhase: hp threshold + traits). `?spawn=<id>` makes every wave one type. CSVs use the "keep" importer so Godot doesn't treat them as translations.
- 2026-10-03: Enemy roster batch 2. Stages pick elites at random from `StageDef.elite_pool` (`elite_picks` per run) after the fixed `elites`, so 13 elites fit in 2 stages and runs vary. `StageDef.enemy_hp_mult` scales enemy and elite hp (stage 2: 1.3) and hp rises 30% each sector loop. `StageDef.mimic_chance` turns that share of scrap piles into Mimics (they don't spin). Mines, meteors and the Mirror Knight's reflected shots damage only through their own scenes; reflected shots and spawned enemies are deferred out of physics callbacks.
- 2026-10-03: One ship per run on every difficulty (Eric). The Spare Hull hangar part (Extra, rare) adds 1-3 ships via the `extra_lives` stat. Game over offers Retry (fire), Hangar (H, or tap the lower third) and Title (pause).
- 2026-10-03: Enemy roster (Eric picked "Everything" from design/enemy-roster.md, built in batches). Batch 1: anything falling (rock chunks, cut rocks, dropped pods) crushes enemies and damages elites through a `Crusher` area; regular enemies get optional `EnemyDef.trait_logic` (`EnemyTrait`: Blink, Dash, Rock Drop) on the unused Spinner, Lancer and Fusewing art; new elites Puppeteer (hurls formation enemies in fast dives, killing it recalls them), Phase Stalker (immune while faded, chases the player's column) and Hive Carrier (towed pod hatches dive swarms; falls as a rock if the carrier dies).
- 2026-10-03: Hangar v3 replaces the materia hangar (Eric). Ships have typed slots (weapon, shield, power, bonus); the weapon sits on the nose and the rest on left, rear and right mounts the player picks. Placement is mild asymmetry: an engine pushes toward the opposite side, a side shield recharges faster. Parts have 3 ranks bought with scrap (no AP or mastery). Combos are a hybrid of rule-based base + element + pattern plus named secret combos; one upgrade tree per ship; the pilot power and the Power slot stay on two buttons. Built in order: slots and placement, then combos, then trees. Old hangar saves keep their pilots and start fresh.
- 2026-10-03: Scrap replaces in-run upgrades (Eric picked "Scrap only"). Destroyed ships leave scrap piles (`EnemyDef.scrap_chance`, `scrap`), which bank straight into the hangar currency (save key `currency`); medals pay scrap too. Every upgrade is bought in the hangar, so Cryo Pulse and Scrap Compactor became modules. UpgradeDef, UpgradePool, SynergyDef and their data are gone; `UpgradeSystem.compute(effects)` only folds effect lists.
- 2026-10-03: Combo modes keep their buffs but no longer change the art style or music (Eric: "too weird at this point"). Done in data: `ComboDef.style` is empty. Stage styles (challenge stages in Outrun) stay.
- 2026-10-03: Elites (Eric): tough named enemies with one counterable trait each, as data (`EliteDef` + an `EliteTrait` script). Frost Shell (regrowing ice layers; heavy or piercing shots crack them 3x faster), Rock Hauler (tows a huge rock that sheds falling chunks; cut the tether from the side), Shield Warden (shields nearby formation enemies; kill it first). They patrol mid-screen and hold the stage until destroyed. `StageDef.elites` places them.
- 2026-10-03: Capture and dual fighter (Eric approved). Wardens (`EnemyDef.can_capture`) fly tractor-beam runs; a captured ship costs a life and rides above its captor. Killing the captor while it attacks frees the ship as a wingman (double fire; a hit takes the wingman, not a life); killing it in formation loses the ship. The captor attacks every 6-10s while holding a ship, so rescues are possible.
- 2026-10-03: No upgrade pick between stages (Eric); stages run back to back. Utility upgrades (freeze, shield, credits, score, speed) now come from larger gold capsules, 25% of drops and every perfect-challenge drop; the rest are bullet capsules. `UpgradeDef.Source` is UTILITY or BULLET; `DifficultyDef.upgrade_choices` and the UpgradePick scene are gone.
- 2026-10-03: Upgrade rework (Eric): upgrades are gentler, in-level drops only change bullets (fire rate, shot speed, damage, homing, extra shots, pierce), and stage cards carry utility (Cryo Pulse freeze, extra shield bubbles, shield recharge, credit multiplier, score, speed, magnet, lives). `UpgradeDef.source` (CARD or DROP) decides where an upgrade can appear. Missiles, wing drones and Wide Spread left the pool (the stats stay for the hangar). Drop chances halved.
- 2026-10-03: Cryo Pulse is an active charge refilled each stage, fired with its own button (C or L; pad Y; tap FREEZE on the HUD), separate from the pilot power (Shift/X/K, pad B/X, two fingers). It freezes enemies and their shots for `freeze_time` seconds; frozen enemies can still be shot. Picked over an automatic trigger so the player chooses the moment.
- 2026-10-03: `credit_mult` stat (Salvage Contract card) multiplies hangar medal payouts (`Hangar._currency_mult`).
- 2026-10-03: Pilots bring one active power on a power button that recharges (Eric picked "Active button" over passive or both). Pilots are bought with credits in the hangar; the first is free.
- 2026-10-03: Hangar credits come only from stage medals, paid once per medal per run and scaled by `DifficultyDef.score_multiplier` (architecture uses one score/currency multiplier). Credits save immediately so a death never loses them.
- 2026-10-03: The hangar is materia style (Eric: "deeper menu, upgrade chain, think Final Fantasy materia"). Frames have slots with linked pairs, and modules level from AP (1 per kill while equipped). Mastery spawns a copy and unlocks a chain module. Blue support modules only work when linked. Effects use the UpgradeDef format and feed `GameState.set_meta_effects`, so the hangar adds no stat code. This replaces the v1 rank tree; old hangar saves reset (credits kept).

- 2026-10-03: Medals are `MedalDef` resources referenced by `StageDef.medal` (instead of a goal id + `medal_currency`), tracked from EventBus counts by `MedalTracker`.
